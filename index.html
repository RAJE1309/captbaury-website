About

Capt. Baury Marine & Offshore Advisors Inc. (O/A Capt. Baury Maritime & Logistics Excellence Inc.) provides specialist marine and offshore consultancy services focused on safe ship operations, barge & towage management, FSO/FPSO/MOPU operations, and environmental protection. Founded by Capt. Rajesh Baury (Master Mariner, UK Class 1 CoC) with 35+ years of experience, we serve shipowners, ports, and logistics operators in the Toronto, Hamilton, and Great Lakes region.

Professional Expertise

  • Offshore towing & anchor handling — deep-sea towage of barges, rigs, and offshore units.
  • Buoy layout & offshore barge operations for oil trading, lightering, and terminal support.
  • FSO, FPSO & MOPU conversion, commissioning & positioning, including First Oil operations and heavy-lift logistics.
  • Dynamic Positioning (DP) systems management, audits, and crew training.
  • Pre-cargo fixture planning, voyage analysis, and charter-party review.
  • Ship-to-ship transfers, tank cleaning, grade changes, and drydocking supervision.

Cargo Expertise

Clean white products: Jet Oil, Low Sulphur Gas Oil, High Sulphur Gas Oil, Kerosene, Naphtha.

Black oils & heated products: High Sulphur Fuel Oil; heated vegetable oils (palm oil, soybean oil, canola oil, palm olein, fatty residues).

Additional Expertise & Key Skills

Fixture & Documentation

Pre-cargo fixture analysis, bills of lading, cargo manifests, and indemnity clauses.

Contamination & Claims

Project Management
