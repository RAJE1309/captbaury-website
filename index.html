About the Company

Capt. Baury Marine & Offshore Advisors Inc. provides executive-level maritime, offshore, energy, and logistics consulting services. The company specializes in tanker and barge operations, marine assurance, SIRE 2.0 readiness, offshore oil trading support, marine waste management, regulatory compliance, and strategic energy logistics.

Led by Capt.
